Study MBBS in Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan offers an ideal balance of top-ranked national government universities, modern simulation facilities, short flight travel from India (only 3.5 hours from Delhi), and remarkably affordable tuition packages.

Duration 5 Years (English Medium)
Tuition Fees ₹2,40,000 - ₹3,80,000 / Year
Travel Time ~3.5 Hours Direct Flight

Why Choose Kazakhstan for MBBS?

✈️

Short Flight Distance

Direct non-stop flights from New Delhi take only around 3.5 hours, making visits during vacations easy and economical.

💰

High Affordability

Complete 5-year MBBS total package is among the lowest globally without sacrificing academic or clinical training quality.

🌐

Global Accreditation

Universities are listed in World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS) and compliant with NMC foreign medical graduate norms.

🍛

Indian Canteen & Mess

Campus hostels feature dedicated Indian messes serving nutritious vegetarian and non-vegetarian daily meals.

Top Medical Universities in Kazakhstan

Almaty, Kazakhstan

Al-Farabi Kazakh National University

Ranked #1 in Kazakhstan and top 200 globally in QS rankings. Renowned faculty of medicine with modern labs.

Approx Tuition: ₹3,60,000 / Yr English Medium
Almaty, Kazakhstan

Asfendiyarov Kazakh National Medical University

Oldest medical school in Kazakhstan (est. 1930) with vast network of university hospitals and clinical departments.

Approx Tuition: ₹3,80,000 / Yr English Medium
Semey, Kazakhstan

Semey State Medical University

High FMGE pass rates, dedicated clinical training hospitals, and large Indian student community.

Approx Tuition: ₹2,60,000 / Yr English Medium
