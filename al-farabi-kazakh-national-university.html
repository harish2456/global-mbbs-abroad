Al-Farabi Kazakh National University

Al-Farabi Kazakh National University (KazNU) is the leading national university of Kazakhstan, recognized as the premier institution of Central Asia. Its Faculty of Medicine and Health offers a modern 5-year English-medium MBBS program with state-of-the-art clinical simulation labs and low tuition fees.

Course Duration 5 Years (English Medium)
Annual Tuition Approx. ₹3,60,000 / Year
Global Standing Top 200 Globally (QS)

University Overview & Academic Highlights

KazNU boasts a vast 100-hectare smart campus in Almaty with dedicated medical research institutes, state-of-the-art anatomy dissection halls, 3D anatomical models, and affiliations with top government municipal hospitals across Almaty city.

⭐ High Global Prestige

Highest ranked university in Central Asia, providing an internationally recognized degree valid across India, USA, and UK.

✈️ 3.5 Hours from India

Almaty is directly connected to New Delhi with daily flights taking just around 3.5 hours.

🍛 Campus Indian Mess

Hostels situated within the secure university campus feature dedicated Indian food messes with Indian cooks.

📜 NMC Compliant 5-Year Curriculum

Full 54-month English instruction plus 12-month clinical internship aligning with FMGL gazette rules.
