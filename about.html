Company Overview

Who We Are

GLOBAL MBBS ABROAD is a dedicated international medical education consultancy established to guide aspiring Indian medical students toward world-class overseas medical universities. We specialize in navigating the complex pathways of foreign medical admissions with total clarity, ethics, and care.

Our Mission in International Medical Education: Every year, over 20 lakh Indian students appear for the NEET examination, yet domestic government seats remain severely limited, while private college capitation fees remain inaccessible for many middle-class families. Our mission is to democratize high-standard medical education by connecting Indian students with accredited, high-ranking government institutions abroad that offer English-medium MBBS programs at reasonable tuition fees.

Our Commitment to Indian Students: We are deeply committed to ensuring that no student embarks on their overseas journey unprepared. From detailed university fee structures to strict adherence to the National Medical Commission (NMC) Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate (FMGL) guidelines, we ensure parents and students have complete peace of mind.

Our Student-Focused Counselling Approach: We never employ generic, one-size-fits-all recommendations. Our counsellors evaluate each student’s academic profile, NEET performance, financial comfort, language preferences, and clinical career ambitions to help them make an empowered, well-informed choice.

Our Evolution

Our Journey

A dedicated path of growth, student support, and continuous commitment to international medical education.

01
Inception & Groundwork

Founding with a Student-First Mission

GLOBAL MBBS ABROAD was established by senior education advisors who recognized the pressing need for transparent, ethical, and structured overseas medical admission guidance for Indian students faced with severe domestic seat competition.

02
Global Alliances

Partnering with NMC-Compliant Government Campuses

We established official direct representation and institutional collaborations with leading government medical universities across Russia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Georgia, Kyrgyzstan, and the Philippines, ensuring complete English-medium instruction and high clinical exposure.

03
Student Welfare Ecosystem

Establishing Complete On-Ground Student Support

Recognizing that parent peace-of-mind is paramount, we expanded our services beyond admission letters to include verified hostel accommodations, hygienic Indian mess setups, local student coordinators, and emergency assistance networks across university towns.

04
Present & Beyond

Ongoing Commitment to Medical Excellence

Today, GLOBAL MBBS ABROAD continues to lead with transparent counselling, assisting hundreds of medical aspirants every academic intake, staying completely aligned with evolving NMC FMGL regulations, and helping future doctors excel globally.
