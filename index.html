Parents Corner

Everything parents need to know about MBBS abroad — safety, academics, costs and more.

Student Abroad Safety

Safe, secure university campuses equipped with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, dedicated Indian wardens, strict anti-ragging protocols, and secure multicultural student environments.

University & Visa Information

100% compliant with National Medical Commission (NMC) regulations, listed in WHO and ECFMG global directories, with end-to-end embassy visa processing support.

Real Food & Affordability

On-campus Indian mess providing hygienic North and South Indian meals. Highly affordable tuition starting from ₹2.5 Lakhs/year with direct university account transfers and no donations.

Hostel & Student Life

Separate boys and girls hostels equipped with 24-hour central heating, high-speed Wi-Fi, laundry facilities, study lounges, and vibrant Indian festive celebrations.

Parent Guidance & FAQs

Transparent guidance for parents on international forex cards, winter clothing requirements, comprehensive medical insurance, and thorough pre-departure orientation sessions.

Stay Connected with Your Child

Dedicated on-ground Indian coordinators, 24/7 parents emergency helpline, regular academic attendance reports, and full support for parent visiting visas.
